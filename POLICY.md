# PRIVACY POLICY - Doge-bot

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

1. WHAT WE COLLECT
- Telegram User ID, Username, First Name
- Your DOGE wallet address you provide for withdrawal
- Investment amount, profit balance, referral count, timestamps
- Transaction ID if you forward it

2. HOW WE STORE IT
Data is stored in a local file (users.json) on our server (Render.com). We do not use external databases.

3. HOW WE USE IT
- To manage your investment balance
- To track referrals
- To process withdrawals
- To contact you regarding your deposit

4. SHARING
We do NOT sell your data. We only share your wallet address when processing a withdrawal on the blockchain (public). We may share data if required by law.

5. DATA RETENTION
We keep your data as long as the bot is active. You can request deletion by contacting @cityhyena, but withdrawal data may remain on the blockchain.

6. SECURITY
We use basic server security but cannot guarantee 100% security. Do not share private keys.

7. YOUR RIGHTS
You can request to see, correct, or delete your data by contacting admin.

8. CHILDREN
This service is not for persons under 18.

9. CONTACT
Privacy questions: @cityhyena
